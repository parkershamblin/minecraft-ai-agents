**Project Sid / PIANO (arXiv:2411.00114; GPT-4o, 10–1000+ agents).** PIANO runs ~10 modules *concurrently at different timescales* against a shared Agent State: non-LLM/cheap — memory (store/retrieve), action awareness (did my action work? — your `ActionAwareness` is this), skill execution, motor execution, and reflex modules (small fast non-LLM nets for immediate threats); LLM-bound — goal generation, social awareness, talking, planning/cognition, and the Cognitive Controller (CC). Arbitration is a *bottleneck*: the CC reads a deliberately compressed slice of agent state, makes ONE high-level decision, and broadcasts it downstream; talk/motor modules are strongly conditioned on that decision so speech and action stay coherent (their fix for the classic "says X, does Y" incoherence). Numbers (all GPT-4o): full architecture averages 17 unique items/agent in 30 min (top agents 30–40, diamonds ~30 min; 49 agents/4h reached ~320 cumulative unique items); removing action awareness measurably slows progression. 30-agent specialization (farmers/miners/guards/etc.) requires the social-awareness module — ablated, role entropy stays low and no long-term relationships form; sentiment-inference correlation 0.807 vs 0.617 without social modules. 29-agent taxation: compliance tracked the 20% constitutional rate and moved bidirectionally (down to 5–10% under anti-tax influencers) only with amendable rules + social/goal modules; baseline architecture couldn't propagate influence at all. 500-agent runs: memes need population density; 20 Pastafarian priests converted steadily across towns over 2h. Nothing here is demonstrated below GPT-4o, but the *concurrency skeleton is model-free*: reflexes and action-awareness are code, and only the CC/goal/social calls hit the LLM.

**VillagerAgent (arXiv:2406.05720; GPT-4-1106 / Gemini Pro / GLM-4, 2–8 agents).** Centralized: LLM task decomposer emits a DAG of subtasks (JSON, predecessor deps) per round; an agent controller dispatches ready nodes to agents; a state manager keeps LLM-summarized long-term state. Vs AgentVerse (both GPT-4): cooking 73.75% vs 29.75% completion; hallucination-failure 18.2% vs 44.4%; token cost 1.79 vs 10.3 (~5.8x cheaper). Construction 36.45%, escape room 73.29% (GPT-4). Ablation: *uniform* agent APIs 56.67% vs heterogeneous 36.67% — capability symmetry simplifies coordination. Scaling: more agents help simple tasks, then diminish on complex ones — explicitly blamed on "increased context length and complexity of generating task graphs." The DAG idea survives small models only if the graph lives code-side; GPT-4-class generation of the graph is the frontier dependency.

**TeamCraft (arXiv:2412.05255; 55k task variants; GPT-4o vs fine-tuned 7B/13B VLA).** Building/clearing/farming/smelting under centralized (one model, all observations, all actions) vs decentralized control. GPT-4o one-shot "failed on almost all test cases" (3D coordinate mapping). TeamCraft-VLA-7B (Vicuna-1.5+CLIP, SFT on procedurally generated expert demos), centralized: building 42%, clearing 64%, farming 36%, smelting 24%; novel goals 0–56%; unseen 4-agent teams collapse to 2–38% — models "ignore the fourth agent." Centralized redundant-work rate 1% vs 15% decentralized. Extraction: an SFT'd 7B *beats a frontier model zero-shot* on embodied multi-agent tasks (same shape as MINDcraft's 0.00→0.28), and centralized dispatch nearly eliminates duplicated work — but agent-count generalization is the weak axis.

**HAS (arXiv:2403.08282; GPT-4V gpt-4-1106-vision-preview, ≤8 agents, MineDojo+mineflayer).** Centralized-planning/decentralized-execution: one Manager (Planner/Describer/Critic/Deployer) auto-organizes groups; each group's conductor (Actor/Curriculum/Critic/Skill) is the *only* agent its workers may talk to (hierarchical, structured comms). Vs single-agent Voyager/STEVE: image-goal search SR 0.76 vs 0.21/0.25 in 27 vs 95/85 iterations; block search 367 vs 81/211 blocks; map exploration 1368 vs 755/696 area. Multi-agent parallelism plus a multimodal task-state memory carries it; frontier VLM throughout.

**S-Agents (arXiv:2402.04578; GPT-4 root planning + GPT-3.5-turbo-16k elsewhere, temp 0; 1 root + 3 workers; Voyager skill library, MC 1.19).** Tree-of-agents (root assigns, leaves execute autonomously and report back) beats chain (29.0 min) and graph (9.3 min) at 7.5 min on mine-50-stones. Org vs solo: 50 logs 5.1 vs 10.2 min; 100 logs 11.1 vs 18.3; 50/100 iron: solo NaN (no progress >40 min) vs 15.3/24.3 min — *collaboration converts impossible-solo sparse-resource tasks into completed ones*. Biggest single result for your stack: **non-obstructive (async, each agent reports to root when done, message pool) 4.0 min vs obstructive round-based relay 29.0 min on the same task** — a ~7x win that is pure runtime architecture, zero model dependency. Scaling: dense resources (stone) don't parallelize (slowest-agent bound, 6.5→7.5 min from N=1→4); sparse (iron) needs N=4.

**MindAgent (arXiv:2309.09971; CuisineWorld + Minecraft transfer).** One centralized dispatcher LLM sees full state and issues per-agent atomic commands (goto/get/put/activate/noop) each timestep. CoS (completion ratio averaged over 5 task-frequency regimes): GPT-4 0.67–0.69; Claude-2 0.31–0.47; **ChatGPT and LLaMA-2-70B: 0.0 — total failure**; no environment feedback drops GPT-4 to 0.311. Agents 2→3→4: 0.673→0.694→0.692 (plateau at 3). Minecraft transfer: GPT-4 CoS 0.581. The starkest scale cliff in the literature: even 70B open weights scored zero on centralized dispatch (2023-era instruct tuning; modern 8Bs are better JSON emitters, but treat frontier-only dispatch results with suspicion).

**CoELA (arXiv:2307.02485; TDW-MAT/C-WAH, not Minecraft).** Five modules: Perception, Memory, Communication, Planning, Execution; LLM used for *when/what to communicate* and high-level plan selection. GPT-4 CoELA beats the MCTS-based Hierarchical Planner by >40% efficiency on C-WAH with emergent economical communication; LLaMA-2 underperforms, but **CoLLAMA — LoRA fine-tune on data collected by their own agents — reaches "promising" performance**: the earliest instance of the SFT-rescue pattern on ~13B. Follow-up CoBel-World (arXiv:2509.21981) adds explicit belief modeling: +4% transport rate on TDW-MAT, 6–28% fewer steps on C-WAH, and **64–79% fewer communication tokens** — more evidence that minimizing NL chat is where gains live.

**2025–2026 additions.** *Parallelized Planning-Acting* (arXiv:2503.03505; Qwen-Plus API, 3/5/10 agents, live MC 1.19.4): dual-thread plan/act with an interruptible **single-slot action buffer — newest plan overwrites the queued one** (independently the same design as your v7 latest-intent-wins), centralized team memory, 790-item recursive skill library. 3 agents vs solo: diamond armor 13.7±4.1 vs 28.3±6.1 min; boss fights: Elder Guardian 100% (5 agents), Wither 100% (10), **Ender Dragon 91.7% SR, 5.2±1.5 min (10 agents)** — proof the dragon is reachable when a deterministic skill library carries execution and the LLM only schedules; speedup sublinear from dependency chains + spatial contention. *Gated Coordination* (arXiv:2604.18975, 2026; GPT-4o/GPT-4-1106 over MINDcraft+VillagerBench, 2–3 agents): escalate-to-team gate = heuristics → cost-sensitive score → LLM only for gray-zone; default-local bias. +11.3/+12.5 pts TSR (21.5→32.8 / 22.04→34.56), steps 184→134 / 145→92, **messages 78→11**, ~145 tokens per structured adjudication vs >1500 free-form; ablation shows the *non-LLM scoring function is the dominant contribution* (TSR 31.4 of 32.8). *TickingCollab* (arXiv:2606.15684, 2026; GPT-5.1/DeepSeek-R1, 2–8 agents, time-pressured tasks): sync vs real-time async success collapses 0.42→0.15, 0.62→0.05, 0.65→0.00 because ~20s inference latency exceeds 25–44s time-to-failure windows — **reactivity can never come from the deliberation loop**. *MECoBench* (arXiv:2606.31966, 2026; VirtualHome, open VLMs 8B–235B incl. Qwen3-VL-8B/32B, Gemma4-31B): two agents add 4–12 pts SR (weaker models gain MORE), inverted-U beyond that; shared memory cuts tokens and beats chat on parallel tasks; communication matters most on sequential dependencies. MINDcraft/MineCollab (arXiv:2504.17950, which you have) remains the direct NL-ablation: detailed NL plan communication *costs* up to 15%.

## Coordination mechanisms ranked by evidence

1. **Asynchronous non-blocking execution + latest-intent-wins buffers** — S-Agents 29.0→4.0 min (7x, GPT-3.5/4); TickingCollab sync≫async collapse; 2503.03505's single-slot buffer. Model-free, strongest cost/benefit, and you've already shipped half of it (v7).
2. **Centralized planner-dispatcher / CPDE hierarchy** — VillagerAgent 73.75% vs 29.75%; HAS SR 0.76 vs 0.21; TeamCraft redundancy 1% vs 15%; MindAgent works only here. Best-evidenced *mechanism*, but every zero-shot instance used GPT-4-class planners.
3. **Gated, structured, minimal communication (blackboard/shared state over chat)** — Gated Coordination +11–12.5 pts with 85% fewer messages, non-LLM gate dominant; MineCollab −15% from detailed NL plans; CoBel-World −64–79% comm tokens; MECoBench shared-memory > chat on parallel tasks. Direct structured-vs-NL ablations beyond MineCollab: Gated Coordination (145-token JSON vs >1500-token free-form) and MECoBench (protocol comparison).
4. **DAG/task-queue decomposition with code-side state manager** — VillagerAgent's wins and its 5.8x token reduction; but LLM-generated graphs are the scaling bottleneck; keep the graph in Postgres, not in the prompt.
5. **Explicit role assignment / capability-uniform teams** — VillagerAgent uniform-API ablation 56.67% vs 36.67%; Sid specialization needs GPT-4o social awareness. Suggestive, not decisive.
6. **Auction/contract-net** — effectively absent from embodied Minecraft literature; no measured wins to report.

**Team-size scaling:** plateaus at 2–4 everywhere (MindAgent 3; MECoBench inverted-U at 2; TeamCraft models drop the 4th agent). Break causes, in evidence order: planner context growth (VillagerAgent), slowest-agent barriers in round-based schemes (S-Agents — fixed by async), spatial contention + dependency chains (2503.03505), comms overhead (MECoBench). Sparse-resource tasks are the exception: N=4 turns iron from impossible to 15.3 min (S-Agents).

|---|---|---|
