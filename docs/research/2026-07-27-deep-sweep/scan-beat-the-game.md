# What Beating Minecraft Actually Requires — Scout Report (2026-07-27)

## 1. Milestone SOTA

**Direct answer: No autonomous agent — LLM or RL — has a published, verified ender-dragon kill from a fresh survival world as of July 2026.** The [Manifold market "AI defeats Ender Dragon before 2026"](https://manifold.markets/AdamK/will-an-ai-minecraft-agent-defeat-t-CPpOcyROzc) resolved NO; the [before-2027 market](https://manifold.markets/AdamK/will-an-ai-minecraft-agent-defeat-t-609shENQnu) sits at ~28% with commenters noting "no autonomous LLM agent has publicly beaten the Ender Dragon end-to-end." No arXiv paper claims it either ([survey sweep](https://github.com/git-disl/awesome-LLM-game-agent-papers)). Published SOTA stalls at the diamond-tier plateau:

| Stage | Best published | Model / conditions | Survives 8B-local-greedy? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood / stone | Saturated: [Optimus-3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.10357v1) SR 0.99/0.95; [Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291) 15.3×/8.5× faster unlock | Optimus-3 = Qwen2.5-VL-7B MoE + VPT action head; Voyager = GPT-4 codegen over mineflayer | Yes — your verbs already do this |
| Iron | Optimus-3 SR 0.55; Voyager 6.4× faster | as above | Yes (you do it today) |
| Diamond | [GITM](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/GITM) 67.5% ObtainDiamond; [DreamerV3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104) first from-scratch RL diamond; [VPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11795) 2.5% diamond *pickaxe* | GITM = GPT-3.5/4 decomposing to **structured actions run by handcrafted controllers** — i.e., a verb executor, not raw control | GITM's *architecture* is yours; its planner was frontier. Planning depth, not dexterity, is the 8B question |
| Diamond pickaxe / diamond gear | [JARVIS-1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05997) 12.5% @60 min (up from VPT's 2.5%); crafts "almost all diamond items incl. diamond chestplate" | Memory-augmented multimodal GPT-class planner over MineDojo | Frontier planner; memory mechanism ports (you have pgvector) |
| Full overworld tech tree (incl. obsidian-dependent enchanting table) | GITM claims "100% of Overworld tech tree" vs 30% for all prior agents combined | **Overworld only** — the claim explicitly excludes Nether/End; structured-action caveat applies | Same as above |
| Obsidian + lit portal → Nether traversal → blaze rods → pearls → stronghold → End | **No published agent has chained any of this from a fresh world.** Benchmarks (MineDojo/[MCU](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.08367)) test "combat blaze"-style tasks with the agent *spawned in the Nether with gear* — never reached organically | — | Open frontier for everyone, including frontier models |
| Dragon kill | **None.** [MineEvolve (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13131v3) and [WISE (2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.12852) still evaluate on MCU iron/diamond/redstone groups | — | — |

Reading the claims correctly: GITM's "ObtainDiamond" is MineDojo's task family, not a survival playthrough — LLM goal decomposition → sub-goals → scripted controllers. Voyager (GPT-4 + mineflayer JS codegen) never entered the Nether and collapses with weaker code-writing models. Optimus-3 is the interesting datapoint for you: a **7B model** drives wood→redstone milestones — but only after 230k-sample SFT + reasoning tuning + GRPO, i.e., training infra you don't have (though its existence supports your ledger→SFT candidate #3). Unpublished/hobbyist: [mindcraft](https://github.com/kolbytn/mindcraft) (LLM+mineflayer, same shape as your stack) has no verified dragon kill; scripted non-learning bots killing the dragon ([Reddit stunt](https://sportskeeda.com/minecraft/news-minecraft-redditor-uses-bot-kill-ender-dragon)) don't count as autonomous agents.

**Implication for the north star: from diamond onward you would not be chasing published SOTA — you'd be extending it.** The blockers are capability plumbing (placement, ranged, dimension travel), which favors your trusted-executor architecture, not model scale.

## 2. Requirements DAG (cautious-player route, 1.21 mechanics)

| Stage | Items needed | NEW capability beyond current verbs | Main dangers | 3–6 villager parallelization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wood/stone/food | 1 crafting table, stone tools, furnace, ~20 food | none | night mobs | 2× wood, 1× stone, 1× hunt/farm |
| Iron age | ~31 iron: full armor 24, shield 1, bucket 3, flint&steel 1, + picks; coal | `use_shield` (executor reflex, offhand); `sleep_bed` — **phantoms spawn after 3 sleepless days**, a reactive threat your 30s tick can't dodge | creepers, cave falls | 2 miners, 1 smelter/crafter, 1 guard/farmer |
| Diamonds | ≥7: pick 3, sword 2, (+2 enchanting table optional). Branch-mine Y=−59 | `place_block` (wall off lava), `use_bucket` (douse lava/water MLG) | lava lakes at Y≤−54 — top killer | pairs mine parallel shafts 4 apart; 1 ferry runner |
| Obsidian + portal | 10 obsidian: diamond pick (~9.4 s/block) **or bucket-casting** (needs no diamond pick — bucket + lava pool + water); light with flint&steel | `place_block`, `use_bucket`, `use_item_on_block` (ignite), `enter_portal` + dimension-aware sessions | lava while casting | casting crew of 2; others start gold prep + potions |
| Nether traversal (fortress) | gold armor piece each (piglin neutrality), blocks ×256 for bridging, shield; fire-res potions optional (→ brewing) | `bridge/scaffold` (place-block under pathfinder), `equip` gold, optionally `bow` for ghasts | ghasts (ranged knockback into lava!), lava seas, bedrock ceiling nav | 2 scout pairs opposite directions; 1 portal-camp keeper |
| Blaze rods | 7–8 rods (12 powder for eyes + brewing stand + fuel) | choke-point building (place-block), shield discipline | blaze fireballs set fire; overwhelm at spawner | 2 fighters rotating at spawner choke, 1 support hauling food/gear |
| Ender pearls | cautious: 14–16 pearls. **Enderman-hunting in a warped forest** (only endermen spawn there) beats bartering: [barter odds are 10/469 ≈ 2.1% for 2–4 pearls ≈ 0.065 pearls/ingot → ~185 gold for 12](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bartering) | gaze-aversion rule (don't look at endermen) or `equip` carved pumpkin; barter fallback = `toss` gold + collect | enderman aggro cascades, teleporting attackers | 3 hunters in warped forest w/ looting sword; 2 gold-miners feed barter fallback in parallel |
| Eyes + stronghold | 14–16 eyes (12 frame slots, ~10% pre-filled each; thrown eyes break 20%); iron pick, ladders/blocks | `throw_eye` + **triangulation math code-side** (two throws from separated points — VoT lesson: spatial truth in executor, never in the 8B) | dig-down into lava/ravine; silverfish spawner | two throwers 500+ blocks apart triangulate in one round; dig team of 2 |
| End fight | bow + ~2 stacks arrows/archer, blocks ×128/climber, pick, slow-fall or water bucket, beds NOT recommended | `bow_ranged` (10 crystals on obsidian pillars each heal the dragon; ~8 shootable), climbing+pillaring for the **2 iron-bar-caged crystals — [cannot be damaged from range](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon)**; **[dragon is immune to arrows while perched — melee only on perch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon)** | crystal explosions, dragon breath, endermen (pumpkins!), void knockback | best team-leverage stage in the game: 2 snipers on uncaged crystals, 1 climber on cages, 3 melee squad rushing head every perch |
| Escape | loot XP, exit portal home | none new | none | — |

Bed-explosion cheese (beds detonate when used in the End) is the speedrunner route; it one-shots the user on mistimed clicks — skip for a cautious multi-villager plan. Crystal destruction order matters (each alive crystal heals the dragon 10 HP/tick range).

**Minimum new verb set, dependency-ordered: `place_block` → `use_bucket` → `use_item_on_block`/`enter_portal` → `equip`/`use_shield` → `toss` → `bow_ranged` → `sleep_bed` → `throw_eye`.** Everything else composes from existing gather/craft/hunt.

## 3. Mineflayer feasibility

Core 1.21 protocol support is landed ([issue #3406](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer/issues/3406)); you already run 1.21.6 in prod, so plugins are the risk surface, not the core.

| Capability | Support | Plugin | Known issues / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free block place | **Core** `bot.placeBlock(ref, face)`; pathfinder auto-scaffolds via `Movements.scafoldingBlocks` | [mineflayer-pathfinder](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer-pathfinder) | The "blockUpdate did not fire within 5000ms" false-negative you already patched in `placeCarried` — generalize that world-state verification to all placement |
| Bucket | **Core** — equip bucket, `bot.lookAt(target)`, `bot.activateItem()` | none needed | No result event; verify via block read (same pattern as ghost-dig detection) |
| Bed | **Core** `bot.sleep(bedBlock)` / `bot.wake()` | none | Rejects if mobs nearby/not night; beds in Nether/End explode on use — a feature only if you want the cheese |
| Bow / ranged | Plugin: ballistic auto-aim solver | [minecrafthawkeye](https://github.com/sefirosweb/minecraftHawkEye); [mineflayer-pvp is melee-only — bows explicitly unsupported (issue #5)](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer-pvp/issues/5); [@nxg-org/mineflayer-custom-pvp](https://github.com/nxg-org/mineflayer-custom-pvp) merges both | Community-maintained; smoke-test on 1.21.6 before pinning (your exact-pin rule applies) |
| Piglin barter | No API needed: `bot.toss('gold_ingot')` near piglin, collect drops after 6 s | none | Wear gold via `bot.equip`; piglins in 1.21 aggro if you open chests near them |
| Portal light + dimension travel | Light: equip flint_and_steel + `bot.activateBlock(obsidian)`. Travel: walk into portal (`setControlState('forward')`), wait for respawn/`spawn` event and `bot.game.dimension` change | core | Oldest known wart: pathfinder treating portal interiors as unwalkable ([issue #709](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer/issues/709)) — drive the final step with raw controls, not pathfinder. **Your real risk is in YOUR stack**: BotSession state, hazard reflexes, and the physicsSimCache all assume one world — key executor state on `bot.game.dimension`, expect full chunk reload, and remember 1:8 coordinate scaling in intents |
| Pathfinding in Nether/End | Works, but tune `Movements` (lava cost, no digging under falling blocks); long lava seas need explicit bridging goals | pathfinder | Your new `MOVE_MAX_DISTANCE`/staging-waypoint gate is exactly the right shape for Nether legs; ghast knockback mid-path is the classic never-settling-promise case your `Promise.race` watchdog already covers |
| Dragon/crystal targeting | Crystals are normal entities (`end_crystal`) — `bot.attack` or hawkeye target; dragon via `bot.attack` during perch (position ≈ 0,0 fountain) | hawkeye for airborne | Dragon's multipart hitbox makes mid-flight melee unreliable — plan melee-on-perch only, matching the wiki mechanic anyway |
| Elytra | exists (mineflayer-elytrafly) | — | Ignore per scope |

**Bottom line:** the literature's ceiling (diamond tier) was reached with exactly your architecture — LLM planner over a trusted verb executor — so nothing published says 8B-local is disqualified from going further; nobody with any model has published the Nether→End chain. The binding constraints are seven missing verbs (all mineflayer-feasible, two need one community plugin), dimension-aware session handling in your own executor, and code-side spatial/triangulation math. The End fight is the single most team-parallelizable stage in the game — a genuine differentiator for a 6-villager fleet that no published single-agent system can exploit.
